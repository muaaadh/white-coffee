How it works

  1. 1

    Join free

    Sign up with your phone number in the customer portal. We add 50 welcome beans to your card.

  2. 2

    Earn beans

    Show your card at the counter. You get 1 bean for every MVR 5, plus a stamp for every handcrafted drink.

    Treat yourself
